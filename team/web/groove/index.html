How it works

Watch it. Count in.
Dance it. Get scored.

This is what you see on the call: your own video, a ghost of the move, and a timeline that fills with colour as you dance. The demo plays one full round.

  1. 01

    Watch the ghost

    Brodie shows the 8-count twice. The ghost stands on your hips at your size, so you just copy it.

  2. 02

    Count-in

    A metronome at the exact tempo. Five, six, seven, eight, and go.

  3. 03

    Dance it

    Every count is checked against a real performance: shape, timing and energy. Keep the hits coming to build a combo.

  4. 04

    One tip, then loop

    You get one clear cue on your weakest bar. Then Brodie loops that part slower until you've got it.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It's a video call. Brodie shows the ghost, the timeline and your score right in the call video, and plays the beat through the call audio.

I only have a laptop webcam. Does that work?

Yes. Brodie only needs to see you from head to hips, and your feet are never scored. If you're sitting or can't step back, tell him and he switches to arms-only.

Can I control it with my voice?

Yes. Say again, slower, faster, next, pause or stop. Ask "how did I do?" for a breakdown, or say a four-digit code to take on a friend's challenge. Raise both hands to say you're ready.

What if something hurts?

Say so and Brodie stops right away. He'll tell you to sit down, rest and drink some water, and to talk to a doctor or someone nearby if it doesn't ease. He never pushes you through pain.
